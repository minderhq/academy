# Hands-On Labs

Fifteen graded labs, LAB-000 through LAB-014, each pairing a task brief with a matching solution page under `solutions/`. Attempt the lab yourself first - the solution pages are the reference implementations, not the starting point. Start with LAB-000 to provision your environment, then follow the IDs in order for a difficulty-sorted path from Docker fundamentals to AI evaluation and safety.

## Available Labs

### [LAB-000: Environment Setup](./LAB-000-ENVIRONMENT-SETUP.md)
Beginner · 1 hour - provision the machine every later lab builds on.

### [LAB-001: Docker & LLM Fundamentals](./LAB-001-Docker-LLM.md)
Beginner · 2 hours - containerize and run your first local LLM.

### [LAB-002: RAG Implementation with Qdrant & Ollama](./LAB-002-RAG-Implementation.md)
Intermediate · 4 hours - build a working RAG pipeline end to end.

### [LAB-003: LoRA Fine-Tuning with QLoRA](./LAB-003-LoRA-FineTuning.md)
Advanced · 4 hours - parameter-efficient fine-tuning on consumer GPUs.

### [LAB-004: Building ReAct Agents](./LAB-004-ReAct-Agent.md)
Advanced · 4 hours - reasoning-plus-acting agents with tool use.

### [LAB-005: GraphRAG Implementation with Neo4j & Qdrant](./LAB-005-GraphRAG.md)
Advanced · 5 hours - combine graph structure with vector retrieval.

### [LAB-006: Train a Small Language Model from Scratch](./LAB-006-Train-Model-From-Scratch.md)
Advanced · 7 hours - tokenizer, pretraining loop and eval, all yours.

### [LAB-007: Production RAG System](./LAB-007-Production-RAG.md)
Advanced · 6 hours - harden RAG for real traffic and real data.

### [LAB-008: Multi-Agent Fleet](./LAB-008-Agent-Fleet.md)
Advanced · 6 hours - orchestrate cooperating agents at scale.

### [LAB-009: Production Deployment](./LAB-009-Production-Deployment.md)
Advanced · 12 hours - the full path from repo to deployed service.

### [LAB-010: DPO Alignment](./LAB-010-DPO-Alignment.md)
Advanced · 6 hours - align a model with direct preference optimization.

### [LAB-011: Multi-Modal AI](./LAB-011-Multi-Modal-AI.md)
Advanced · 9 hours - vision-language systems in practice.

### [LAB-012: Audio AI](./LAB-012-Audio-AI.md)
Advanced · 7 hours - speech and audio pipelines end to end.

### [LAB-013: Advanced Function Calling](./LAB-013-Advanced-Function-Calling.md)
Advanced · 7 hours - reliable tool use beyond the basic demo.

### [LAB-014: AI Evaluation & Safety](./LAB-014-AI-Evaluation-Safety.md)
Advanced · 6 hours - evaluate and guardrail models before they ship.
