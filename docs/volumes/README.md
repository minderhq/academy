# The Seven Volumes: Curriculum Map

The complete Minder Academy curriculum in seven volumes, from bare-metal infrastructure to production mastery. Read them in order for the full path; each volume page lists its modules, practice files and assessments so you always know where you are and what comes next.

## The Volumes

### [Volume 1: Infrastructure Fundamentals](./VOLUME-1-Infrastructure.md)
Beginner - "Building the Foundation": set up your AI lab and run your first local LLMs.

### [Volume 2: AI/ML Foundations](./VOLUME-2-AI-Foundations.md)
Intermediate - "The Mathematics of Intelligence": calculus, linear algebra and the frameworks behind AI.

### [Volume 3: LLM Internals & Architecture](./VOLUME-3-LLM-Internals.md)
Advanced - "Understanding the Transformer": how LLMs work internally.

### [Volume 4: Quantization & Optimization](./VOLUME-4-Quantization.md)
Advanced - "Bending the Brain": maximize your hardware to run larger, faster models.

### [Volume 5: Model Adaptation](./VOLUME-5-Model-Adaptation.md)
Advanced - "Making Models Your Own": fine-tune and align LLMs for your specific use cases.

### [Volume 6: Data Nexus: RAG & Memory](./VOLUME-6-Data-Nexus.md)
Advanced - "Beyond Training Data": give LLMs access to external knowledge and long-term memory.

### [Volume 7: Production Mastery](./VOLUME-7-Production-Mastery.md)
Advanced - "From Lab to Production": deploy, scale and monitor AI systems in production environments.
